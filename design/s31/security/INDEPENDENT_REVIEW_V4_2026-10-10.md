## V4 two-call inspection needs a cross-path check

**Priority: P1 before V4 is called authoritative for the executable two-call profile. Current consequence: an assurance gap, not a demonstrated forged proof.**

[`inspectTwoCall`](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/bounded_compiled_binding.zig) compiles with `compileDirectBoundedWithSpans` at lines 151–160, builds a pair Plan from those extracted addresses at lines 162–175, then compares the V4 roster with V3 handles **constructed from the same bounded circuit** at lines 195–197 and 238–278. The native pair verifier instead derives its source binding with `compileDirectPairWithSpans` in [`pair_source_binding.derive`](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/pair_source_binding.zig) at lines 40–70; [`verifySourceBound`](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/pair_native_package.zig) uses that derivation at lines 181–193. No current V4 inspection check compares the bounded Plan or preprocessed root to that live verifier derivation. The two lowering modes share much of `compileWithSpansMode`, but their mode branches and endpoint extraction differ. A future change could make the bounded inspector and live verifier disagree while the inspector remains internally self-consistent.

The minimal cross-path control is to derive the V3 live binding from the **same literal source and pinned AIR bytes** inside `inspectTwoCall`, then require equality of source SHA-256, canonical IR SHA-256, call count, every call ID/round count/constant/eight endpoint addresses, preprocessed root, and trace log. Compare the selected bundled AIR identity and circuit geometry as well. Add a negative test that perturbs the bounded endpoint mapping or root while holding the live V3 derivation fixed and requires rejection. This is a useful differential check, not a compiler theorem: both modes still share parser, canonical IR and compiler code. To make V4 authoritative, the native prover and verifier must also derive and check the V4 roster and bind its versioned digest before witness commitments. The current V4 API explicitly accepts no proof bytes and does none of that.

## Source identity and metadata scope

The V4 source-plan generator takes [`CircuitFacts`](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/bounded_component_manifest.zig) from its caller at lines 73–82 and 118–149. Its `fromSource`/`matchesSource` checks rederive calls and public outputs, but they cannot authenticate a caller's selected AIR identity, circuit widths/constraint count, fixed-column order or preprocessed root. `inspectTwoCall` improves this for the two-call inspection by taking selected AIR facts from `directPair` and calculating a root from compiled endpoints (bounded binding lines 163–197). It does not do so for one or three through eight calls, which have no native proof path.

The V4 manifest's `Call` entries and native component binding hashes include call IDs, source node IDs, rounds and constants, but **not endpoint addresses** (bounded manifest lines 54–60 and 383–399). The standalone V4 digest binds a caller-supplied preprocessed root and generated component geometry; the separate `TwoCallInspection.topology` carries actual endpoint arrays, and `matchesTwoCallInspection` compares them (bounded binding lines 201–219). A consumer of only the V4 manifest or its digest would therefore lack an explicit per-call endpoint map. A future admitted manifest should either serialize the source-derived addresses in ordered call entries and check them against the compiled/native Plan, or make the root-to-address derivation an enforced part of verifier admission. A rehashed V4 artifact alone is not an endpoint authority.

The V4 `native_template_sha256` hashes the pair-boundary, tagged-chip and tagged-bridge source files (bounded manifest lines 370–399). It is accurately described as an **AIR template** identity. It does not bind the native pair proof scheduler or verifier implementation; the V3 pair component bindings additionally hash `s31_pair_proof_source` in [`pairNativeBinding`](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/component_manifest.zig) at lines 621–637. Treat V4 template hashes as audit metadata until a versioned native schedule and transcript bind all executable dependencies. No proof substitution was found in the present source-pinned V3 verifier, whose source and AIR are embedded and whose proof check reconstructs its own Plan.

## Formal claims and remaining soundness premise

The new [`TaggedPairSourceCorrespondence.lean`](../../../formal/s31/S31/Gadgets/Air/TaggedPairSourceCorrespondence.lean) models public output Gate events at `3+i`, fixed `u` at address 2, the actual five-claim fold, the bridge's sixteen-row next mask, raw bridge paired fractions and chip signed events. The modeled native formulas agree with the inspected fixed profile. Theorems `source_bridge_claim_eq_endpoint_events` and `source_chip_claim_eq_transition_events` explicitly assume every relevant logical-row AIR residual, nonzero denominators, and a source-shaped row permutation (lines 439–596). `source_chip_arithmetic_residuals_sound` similarly assumes all four arithmetic residuals (lines 606–621). The [formal audit](../../../formal/s31/S31/Gadgets/Air/TAGGED_PAIR_BOUNDARY_AUDIT.md) correctly states that verifier/PCS/FRI acceptance implying these logical-row premises, quotient-mask correspondence on the larger evaluation domain, exact Zig bit reversal, and transcript challenge distribution remain open. These Lean results should continue to be described as conditional source-shaped algebra, not as a full verifier soundness proof.

The source-pinned CLI accepts caller-supplied public words (pair verifier lines 15–23). A relying party must fix expected public words independently of a prover-provided statement. The bridge also commits private endpoints without blinding (engine `tagged_pair_bridge.zig::writeBase`, lines 51–70), so this profile does not provide witness confidentiality. Both are already stated in the prior review and are not new false-proof admissions.

**Conclusion:** no false public-claim acceptance was found by this static pass. The concrete next gate is a differential V4-to-live-V3 source/Plan/root check, followed by native V4 transcript admission if the artifact is promoted beyond inspection.

## Second pass on `d566ede`: inspection gap closed

The new public `inspectTwoCall` obtains a live V3 binding through the same `pair_source_binding.derive` used by the native pair verifier, then calls the private `compareLivePairBinding` before returning V4 metadata ([bounded binding](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/bounded_compiled_binding.zig), lines 153–215 and 224–270). That comparison checks literal-source and canonical-IR digests; both call IDs, rounds, constants and all eight input/output addresses per call; the preprocessed root and trace log; the selected AIR's identity and geometry; and exact equality of the whole generated V3 pair manifest. The bounded circuit can no longer validate itself as a substitute for the live V3 lowering. This **closes the cross-path inspection finding above** for the V3-admitted two-call source grammar. It is a differential code check, not an independent compiler proof, because both paths still share parser, canonicalization and compiler implementation.

V4 call entries now carry optional compiled endpoints, and the typed V4 digest encodes a presence tag and all eight addresses when present ([bounded manifest](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/bounded_component_manifest.zig), lines 52–66 and 320–332). The two-call inspection attaches addresses derived from the circuit and requires them to equal the V3 native pair calls ([bounded binding](../../../src/frontends/s31/runtime/bounded_compiled_binding.zig), lines 215–225 and 308–320). `matchesTwoCallInspection` regenerates the compiled form; `matchesSource` regenerates the source-only form with absent endpoints. The new negative controls mutate a compiled endpoint and forced live V3 fields including Plan address, source/IR digest, root, trace log and selected AIR identity/geometry. Four focused Zig targets reportedly passed on this patch; this independent second pass inspected source only and did not rerun those jobs.

The remaining limits are unchanged: V4 is not in a native key or Fiat–Shamir transcript, and no V4 proof verifier consumes it. Source-only `CircuitFacts` remain caller-supplied, and one or three through eight calls still have no native scheduler or proof admission. The V4 native component hash identifies AIR templates and call parameters; the complete inspected digest now also includes endpoint addresses, but neither digest authenticates a distributed executable. The source-pinned V3 verifier remains the only reviewed executable pair path, with expected public words and executable authenticity supplied by the relying party. The Lean source lemmas remain conditional on logical-row AIR, PCS/FRI and challenge premises. No false-proof acceptance or metadata-substitution path was found in this static second pass.
