# V13 topology and call-count prospective result

**Frozen result:** the single V13 run passed every prespecified gate. The protocol and new sources were committed before any V13 native package build at isolated-worktree commit `d557d98e44a1ffd2e7c0568faf7fe65406ec7df4`, protocol SHA-256 `f9b5defa7a6a886e7622d73ac7afa2a503812bfcbec145088ec9903eabce0cc4`. V13 used exactly the same pinned production source base, compiler fingerprint, engine gitlink, host, and `/usr/bin/time` wall/RSS method as V12. V12's measured result was excluded from V13 prediction; only its frozen predictions and a prespecified extra-call rule were used. The V11 frozen failure and V12 frozen pass remain unchanged.

Three previously unseen two-branch circuit programs compiled to their exact frozen native geometry: N1 `[12,12,4]`, N3 `[12,12,4,5,4,4,4]`, and N4 `[12,12,4,5,4,4,4,6,4]`. All sources had 2,048 live packed multiplication nodes, with two 1,024-node branches joined before the chip calls. Builds and manifest checks finished before any proof. The runner then recorded claim-0 post-build and immediate-repeat costs outside the score, followed by 60 scored claims in the frozen interleaved order. All **66/66 honest proofs** verified; all **66/66 exact ABI-valid changed-result statements** failed at the native proof layer. The independent audit checked every saved source, claim, changed statement, proof, binary, native manifest, phase report, timing endpoint, and physical scored order, then replayed all 132 verifier invocations.

Pooled one-draw wall coverage was **58/60 (96.67%)**, above the frozen ≥49/60 gate. All three observed sample-of-20 wall medians fell in the same frozen 90% median band `[114.35,245.88]` ms, above the ≥2/3 gate. Each source passed wall median error ≤30%, one-draw hits ≥14/20, proof median error ≤10%, proof interval hits ≥16/20 (observed 20/20, 20/20, 19/20), and prover/verifier median RSS error ≤30%.

The frozen rule assigned **zero non-PoW wall premium per extra chip call**, because the positive part of the V12 predicted N2-minus-N1 residual difference was zero. The observed medians of whole wall minus both reported native PoW phases were **92.43, 94.34, and 95.02 ms** for N1/N3/N4. Their spread is only 2.59 ms, while FRI PoW medians were **75.78, 45.19, and 98.76 ms**. The differences among the whole-wall medians therefore mostly follow stochastic PoW in this sample. This phase split is consistent with a small marginal call cost for these particular chip rounds and native package, but does not establish a universal zero-cost call rule. The broad V12 PoW predictive bands and only 20 claims/source make the wall coverage gate weak evidence of general calibration.

Package-build wall was separate from proof wall: **79.32, 76.28, and 73.83 seconds** for N1/N3/N4. Claim-0 first verified wall was **1.181, 1.007, and 0.939 seconds**; immediate repeats were **161.45, 174.64, and 119.86 ms**. These build and cold costs were excluded from the scored warm medians according to the pre-native protocol. They remain material for low-volume proving workflows.

The V13 pass extends the *local* V12 cost check to one forked circuit topology and N3/N4 call geometry on the pinned host and revision. It still does not establish reliability over arbitrary S31 programs, trace shapes, hardware, compiler revisions, or longer calls. Proof bytes and RSS were fitted by fragile empirical contrasts, and observed prover RSS is consistently above its forecast. Nothing here establishes a Cairo speedup or cryptographic soundness; the verifier controls test native proof binding for these claims only.
