# V10 one-call same-geometry cost transfer: frozen gates pass

The single prospective V10 run passed **all 12 frozen gates** across three fresh one-call source/claim families. The result supports local transfer of the geometry-aware predictor to new source instances with previously measured circuit/chip trace log pairs. It does **not** establish general prediction on unseen geometry: all three log pairs were fitted from the failed V9 transfer and the V10 sources have related operator skeletons. V9 remains a failed 6/12 transfer of the V8 model; V6 remains integrity-invalidated and unscored. There was no V10 rerun or holdout refit.

The [model](language/mixed-cost-v10-geometry-model.json), [protocol](language/mixed-cost-v10-geometry-transfer-protocol.json), [source roster](language/mixed-cost-v10-geometry-transfer-sources/), scripts, predictions, and pass limits were committed as `a4230b3` and pushed before the native run. The intervals were frozen before measurement. The 100% proof coverage indicates a conservative interval for these three instances; it is not evidence of calibrated 90% coverage across arbitrary programs. The model included a shared max-trace-log proof-size jump of 15,362.75 bytes, an additional circuit-log contrast of 2,060 bytes, and a nine-source FRI PoW mixture. This is a small empirical fit, not a causal decomposition of serialized proof bytes.

The result retains source/claim/proof hashes, every timing endpoint and native phase report, native manifests, and descriptive package-build and first-claim times. Each source had 80 scored claims plus a first-post-build claim and its immediate repeat. The package builds took 74.76–76.82 seconds each; build and claim-zero times were not scored.

All **246 honest proofs** verified with their typed public statements, and all **246 changed public results** were rejected at `InvalidMixedInteractionNonce`. A separate [independent audit](language/mixed-cost-v10-geometry-independent-audit.json), committed after the freeze without changing scored code, additionally reconstructed and compared the exact ABI-valid changed-result file for every claim, checked source/assignment/statement/proof bytes and native reports, recomputed all scores, and replayed all 246 native proofs and 246 negative controls. Both audits passed.

The raw 53 MB package/proof corpus is local at `/tmp/s31-v10-geometry-transfer-a4230b3`. This evidence is one host, one engine/compiler version, and three related one-call programs at already observed geometry.
