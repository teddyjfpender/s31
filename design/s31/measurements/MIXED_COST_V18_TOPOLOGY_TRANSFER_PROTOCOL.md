## New computations and exact admission

Each source has one public four-lane M31 input. It makes 8 or 32 shifted/scaled seeds, then a live chain on every branch. At step `i` on branch `b`, the chain multiplies by its fixed shift when `(i+b) mod k = 0`; otherwise it adds the shift. A balanced addition tree joins all branches. Six or seven sequential chip recurrences then apply `z <- z² + c` for independently specified round counts and constants. The public output scales the last recurrence value and adds the first branch shift. An independent Python M31 oracle evaluates all four lanes of every frozen claim and checks the language interpreter before freeze.

For example, `calls6_log13_b8` starts branch zero with `shift0 = x + 401`, `seed0 = 43 * shift0` modulo `p = 2^31−1`. Step zero multiplies `seed0 * shift0`, steps one and two add `shift0`, and step three multiplies again. Eight branches of 512 operations each join into one value. Calls 0–5 then run `(4096,1024,256,64,2048,512)` rounds with constants 257–262. This is source code generated by `protocol.py`, not an assumed AIR profile.

## Model frozen before V18 native data

The training ledger classifies **V11, V15, and V17 as failures** and V12, V13, V14, and V16 as local passes; their exact protocol/result/score SHA-256 inputs are in the canonical model. The log-13 and log-14 baselines are V17 `calls4_log13` and `calls4_log14` observed scored medians. The positive part of `(V17 calls8_log13 − V17 calls4_log13)/4` is the added-call step, computed separately for non-PoW whole-process wall, proof bytes, prover/verifier RSS, and four native package phases. An N6 prediction adds two such steps to its matched log-size baseline; N7 adds three. This is an intentionally simple, falsifiable average-call rule: V17's extra four calls have different round lengths from the new extra calls, and a log-13 increment may not transfer to log14 or a new branch mix.

The proof interval retains V16's 9.846% relative half-width, calibrated after V15 and used unchanged in V17. Warm whole-process wall adds V12's one-draw stochastic PoW/residual quantiles to the new non-PoW center. V17's N8 median fell below its frozen sample-of-20 band despite 17/20 one-draw hits; accordingly, **before V18 timing** the sample-of-20 median deviations from V12's p50 are scaled by 1.5. The one-draw band and separate FRI/interaction PoW predictions remain V12's. This expansion is training on V17's failed wall outcome, not prospective V17 success. The V18 per-source wall median error gate is 35%, also declared after the 30.52% V17 miss; it remains too loose to establish general cost reliability on its own.

## One run, proof controls, and conjunctive gates

After exact admission, claim zero is proved twice per source. Claim indices 1–20/source are shuffled into 60 interleaved scored tasks using seed `202610130183`. Every honest proof must emit its exact public statement and pass an external native verifier. An exact ABI-valid changed public result must be rejected at `InvalidMixedInteractionNonce`. The independent audit checks saved binaries, manifests, source/claim/proof hashes, phase reports, physical process timing order and OS RSS endpoints; with `--native`, it replays all 66 honest and 66 changed-result verifier calls.

Frozen gates are conjunctive: whole verified wall median relative error ≤35%/source; nominal 90% one-draw wall hits ≥14/20/source and ≥49/60 pooled; sample-of-20 wall median in its frozen 90% band for ≥2/3 sources; non-PoW median error ≤20%; each of four native package-phase median errors ≤30%; proof median error ≤10% and proof interval hits ≥16/20/source; prover and verifier median peak-RSS errors ≤30% each. A cold or scored row with nonpositive reported PoW or nonpositive measured wall minus reported PoW is rejected before score arithmetic. Any manifest, source, proof-control, or scored gate failure is preserved as **FAIL**, with no rerun or retuning.

One host, three related sources, broad stochastic bands, and unmodeled build/cold costs bound the inference even if all V18 gates pass. V17 remains failed. This cost study does not test cryptographic soundness or establish a Cairo speedup.
