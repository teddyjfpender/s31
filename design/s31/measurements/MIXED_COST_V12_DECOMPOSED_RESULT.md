# V12 decomposed unseen-geometry prospective result

**Result:** the single frozen V12 run passed every prespecified gate on this host and source family. This is a local transfer check, not a general whole-prover cost guarantee. The protocol was committed **before any V12 native package build** at isolated-worktree commit `0ae51bde2de1c1dc3a06be423bb52167ee5f9080`, protocol SHA-256 `93c14e4a6d72a75dcf289d2d09f396f064b1b6ec5cf35133f1b5c7f3ab7d839f`. Production source base was `7e90b5833eecb893c6db5f260e5d1cfc69cd230f` with its pinned engine gitlink and compiler/host/tool hashes. V11's failed 14/16 score was an explicitly declared fit input; V12 sources and claims were new.

The four native manifests exactly matched the frozen log-12 geometry `[9,12,4]`, `[12,4,4]`, `[12,12,4]`, and two-call `[9,12,4,5,4]`. The runner built and admitted all four packages before any proof. It then made one claim-0 post-build observation and immediate repeat per source, followed by the 80 scored claims in the frozen randomized interleaving. All **88/88 honest proofs** verified, and all **88/88 exact ABI-valid changed-result statements** failed at the native proof layer. A separate audit recomputed the score from saved files and replayed all 176 verifier invocations with the saved binaries. It checked exact sources, claims, negative statements, proofs, binaries, native phase reports, process timing endpoints, geometry, and physical scored order.

All four observed 20-claim wall medians fell in their separately frozen 90% median bands. Pooled one-draw wall coverage was **78/80 (97.5%)**, above the frozen ≥67/80 gate. Per-source proof-size interval coverage was 90%, 90%, 100%, and 100%, above ≥80%. Prover and verifier median RSS errors were each below 30% for every source; prover RSS was consistently underpredicted by about 14–20%, so this gate passes without implying a tight memory model. All per-source wall median error, wall coverage, proof median error, proof coverage, and RSS gates passed; both pooled/median-band gates passed.

The explicit PoW decomposition helps interpret the wall score. Predicted median FRI PoW was 73.47 ms for each shape; observed medians were 71.44, 59.05, 62.91, and 66.76 ms. Predicted median interaction PoW was 1.20 ms; observed medians were 1.29, 2.44, 1.80, and 0.63 ms. Median wall minus both reported PoW phases was 46.22, 90.80, 91.79, and 46.27 ms. These are process-wall residuals under the same `/usr/bin/time` RSS wrapper used in the V11 fit; they are not an isolated native-phase measurement. One-draw PoW and wall bands are wide because nonce-search duration is stochastic and the rate model uses four V11 source blocks. Their high coverage should not be read as strong calibration evidence.

Build wall was recorded separately: 77.22, 74.07, 74.54, and 74.73 seconds for chip, circuit, joint, and serial packages. Immediate post-build claim-0 verified wall was 1.054, 1.050, 1.173, and 0.883 seconds; immediate repeats were 70.00, 220.91, 338.81, and 66.91 ms. Both claim-0 observations were excluded from the 20-claim scored warm process wall, exactly as frozen. The build and cold costs are material for workflows with few proofs and must not be hidden behind warm median results.

The V12 pass supports the frozen predictor only for this host, source family, source/compiler/engine revision, and wall collection method. Four source geometries and 20 scored claims each are insufficient to claim general reliability. The model's source-level exponential/Gamma PoW assumption, cross-shape log extrapolation, proof-byte increments, and RSS contrasts remain empirical and fragile. The run does not measure a Cairo comparison, prove security, or establish faster proving against another stack. The earlier V11 frozen failure remains a failure; reclassifying it as V12 training did not change its score.
