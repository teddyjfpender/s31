# V14 multiple-long-call and log-13 prospective result

**Frozen result:** the one V14 run passed every prespecified score gate, with an important qualification: the log-13 whole-wall median error was **27.70%**, close to the ≤30% cutoff. The canonical protocol was committed **before any V14 native package build** at isolated-worktree commit `230e7ddad0ee5ec338c70651a4f43a062acdf269`, SHA-256 `9e1acbf57aefa35544ef7761b2c2cd03894926d29f397ce5c323abbe61c4df24`. The V11 failed score and V12/V13 passed scores were declared training; their original scored artifacts were not changed. V14 used the same pinned production source base, compiler fingerprint, engine gitlink, host, and `/usr/bin/time` wall/RSS method as V12/V13.

The native manifests exactly admitted all three new geometries, including **chip log 13**: 2×4,096 rounds `[12,12,4,12,4]`, 3×4,096 rounds `[12,12,4,12,4,12,4]`, and 8,192+4,096 rounds `[12,13,4,12,4]`. All three packages were built and manifested before any proof. The runner then measured claim 0 and its immediate repeat per package outside the score, followed by 60 frozen interleaved scored claims. **66/66 honest proofs** verified and **66/66 exact ABI-valid changed-result statements** failed at the native proof layer. The independent audit checked every saved source, claim, changed statement, proof, binary, manifest, native phase report, timing endpoint, and physical scored order, and replayed all 132 verifier invocations.

Pooled one-draw wall coverage was **56/60 (93.33%)**, above the frozen ≥49/60 gate. All three observed sample-of-20 wall medians lay within their separately frozen 90% bands, above the ≥2/3 gate. Every source met wall median error ≤30%, one-draw hits ≥14/20, proof median error ≤10%, proof interval hits ≥16/20 (all were 20/20), and prover/verifier median RSS errors ≤30%. The high wall coverage owes in part to wide stochastic PoW bands and is weak evidence of general calibration.

The prespecified model decomposed wall into non-PoW process work plus stochastic FRI and interaction PoW. Its predicted **non-PoW centers** were 96.475, 93.337, and 94.245 ms for log-13 N2, log-12 N2, and log-12 N3. Observed medians of wall minus both reported native PoW phases were **96.514, 92.821, and 94.819 ms**: differences of +0.039, −0.517, and +0.573 ms. This is a useful local diagnostic for multiple long calls and log 13, although non-PoW residual accuracy was not a scored gate and the phase subtraction includes process overhead. By contrast, observed FRI PoW medians were **41.87, 55.64, and 42.47 ms** versus the frozen predictive one-draw median **73.47 ms**; this accounts for most of the whole-wall median overprediction. The log-13 observed sample median was near the low edge of its frozen sample-of-20 band. No refit or new scoring rule was applied.

Package-build wall was reported separately as **79.24, 73.67, and 73.13 seconds**. First post-build claim-0 verified wall was **954.26, 1,026.82, and 986.69 ms**, with immediate repeats **136.38, 207.28, and 158.13 ms**. They were excluded from the warm score as frozen. The protocol deliberately had **no build or cold-start prediction gates**: prior builds and first-proof observations are one-shot data confounded by compiler cache and cold process behavior. These costs remain material for workflows with few proofs and must not be hidden behind warm medians.

This pass extends the local cost check to two or three long chip calls and a log-13 component on one pinned host/revision. It does not establish broad reliability across hardware, compilers, arbitrary S31 programs, or much larger traces. The V11→V12 chip log-step transfer was confounded by different source constants; proof/RSS forecasts remain empirical. The near-threshold log-13 wall error and run-to-run PoW variation warrant a more independent distributional validation. The result does not measure a Cairo speedup or establish cryptographic soundness beyond the native verifier controls for these claims.
